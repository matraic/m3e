# 📍 M3E Roadmap

Explore what's in development and what's next for M3E. Priorities may evolve, and the work outlined here is not tied to specific releases or dates.

Looking for what's already shipped? See the [changelog](https://matraic.github.io/m3e/#/changelog.html) for release history.

Have an idea for a component or improvement? [Submit an enhancement request](https://github.com/matraic/m3e/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) and share what you'd like to build.

## 🚧 In Progress

- **Prebuilt Fields** — Ready-to-use fields such as `m3e-text-field`, `m3e-date-field`, and `m3e-combobox`, without manually composing `m3e-form-field`.

## 🔮 Planned

- **Numeric and masked inputs** — Standalone inputs for use with `m3e-form-field` and as building blocks for prebuilt fields.
- **Scroll view** — Overscroll stretch and auto-hiding scrollbars.
- **Motion engine** — A reusable `@m3e/web/motion` module for components and applications, with primitives for FLIP transitions, animation-frame drivers, springs, and gesture-driven motion.
